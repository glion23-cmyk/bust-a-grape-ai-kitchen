# THE MANDATE

Make BUST A GRAPE a fucking banger: instantly legible, physically delicious, difficult to put down, and unmistakably its own thing.

Claude Opus and Gemini own the result together. Design, physics, controls, gameplay, difficulty, feedback, pacing, match flow, replay loop, interface, mobile feel, sound, performance, personality, and code are all fair game. Do not wait for a feature list. Play the current game, find what keeps it from being undeniable, and improve the real build.

This must remain BUST A GRAPE: an original landscape artillery food fight; wheel-high walking grape-bunch Pips; compact illegal Kansas harvest launchers; dirt, juice, broadcast swagger, and Night Harvest League attitude. The locked identity and legal fence already recorded in `STATUS.md` and `docs/` remain the ground under the work.

## New non-negotiable: dimensional play

The current Canvas game is a prototype, not an engine prison. The playable experience must move meaningfully beyond flat 2D. Use true 3D or mechanically meaningful 2.5D: dimensional launchers and Pips, depth-aware terrain or arenas, physical camera movement, lighting/material response, or gameplay that uses depth. A stack of parallax planes, fake perspective painted into sprites, or a 2D game wearing a shader does not satisfy this requirement.

Keep artillery decisions readable from a mostly side-on broadcast camera. Dimensionality should amplify aim feel, impacts, terrain drama, ceremonies, and personality—not turn the game into a free-camera simulator or a sci-fi tank HUD.

The migration may be staged, but every round must leave a runnable game. Prefer a thin vertical slice that proves the new feel before converting every asset and menu.

## Banger bar

- The first meaningful decision happens within five seconds.
- A shot has anticipation, a readable flight, a violent/comic payoff, and a fast path back to agency.
- Skill lives in prediction, manipulation, timing, and risk—not grind or random punishment.
- Rematch friction approaches zero; short sessions create “one more lot” pressure.
- The four ammo lots create genuinely different tactical stories.
- On a representative phone in landscape, touch targets remain reachable, frame pacing remains stable, text remains readable, and effects degrade gracefully.
- Kansas cyberpunk gangster-cowboy flavor comes through machinery, ritual, language, audio, and broadcast framing—not neon identity glow or generic cyber clutter.

The partner relationship is mandatory:

- Read the other model's latest directive, gate, or handoff before choosing work.
- Build on good partner work; call out weak work plainly and fix it.
- Prefer a playable, testable improvement over a pitch deck.
- Leave the next model a clean seam, a real question, and honest evidence.
- Never optimize for agreement. Optimize for the game.

There is intentionally no prescribed feature backlog. The pair must discover the right game together.
