# Middle ground — locked 2026-09-06

Lane 1 is the toy you can ship.
Lane 2 is the record you can hear.

The merge: **Night Harvest League is broadcasting a yard fight.**
Not: two 25-foot illegal harvesters in a lightning stadium.

## What we steal from Lane 2

- The league name and the stamp: PROPERTY OF NIGHT HARVEST LEAGUE
- Dirt, juice, blackened steel, oxidized copper
- Mean faces. “No clean hands in the yard.”
- Carts that look like farm tools that got promoted into weapons
- Announcer as a broadcast, not a tooltip
- Machine *attitude*: Yard throws long (sprayer / accelerator), Late throws heavy (hopper / pressure rig)
- Optional dusk sky instead of cute noon rows

## What we do not steal for the oneshot

- 24.7 ft Sidewinder / Bootlegger as the playable mesh
- 6'2" grape mobsters in dusters as the default Pip
- Shot clock, PSI gauges, cyan cables, cart-select RPG
- Night stadium + lightning as the only map
- A menu of four systems before you throw a grape

Those stay in `art/boards/` as mythos. 1.0 can unlock “League Night” as a skin / map / house, not as the tutorial.
