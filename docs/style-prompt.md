# Style-board prompt
Paste into the other AI. Boards come back into `art/boards/`.

```
Make a style board / art bible page for an original indie game called BUST A GRAPE.

This is NOT a request to copy ShellShock Live, Worms, Angry Birds, or Minions. Original side-view 2D artillery food-fight. Adult, mean, funny. Farm filth + wine snobbery + sports-announcer energy. Not cute. Not educational wine country. Not a tank-game sci-fi HUD.

LOCKED LOOK
- Side-on 2D, chunky readable silhouettes, painted-flat with a little dirt and juice, not chibi, not vector farm-app, not 3D renders.
- Palette only: dirt #3A2A1C, oxblood #6B1C2A, cream #F3E6C8, juice purple #5A1638, ink #1A100C, late sky #8AA7B8 fading to #C4B49A. No yellow hero characters. No neon.
- Type: condensed sports gothic for titles and buttons. Cream on wine-dark for announcer lines. One smashed berry used in the letterforms of BUST A GRAPE.

CHARACTERS CALLED PIPS
Walking bunches of grapes with arms, legs, a stem, tiny boots, and a serious face on the front berry. They are a naval gun crew who happen to be fruit. They run ornate launch ceremonies, then throw other grapes.
NOT Minions: no yellow, no goggles, no denim overalls, no banana, no capsule one-eye body, no stolen faces or names.
NOT rats or rodents of any kind.
Scale: a Pip comes up to the cart’s wheel.

WHAT TO PUT ON THE BOARD (one wide poster or 12 tiles, labeled)

A. Palette strip + dirt / juice / brushed metal / grape-skin material chips.
B. “Yes” thumbnail of the whole playfield: two harvest carts on a chalk-dirt hill, late sky, juice stains, dotted grape arc.
C. “No” strip: Minion, rat, cute farm sticker, sci-fi tank HUD — each X’d out.
D. Yard cart side-view (oxblood sprayer / buggy) and Late cart (dark green-gray), both readable at small size.
E. Pip turnaround: front, 3/4, side. Merlot, cab, and green colorways.
F. Four Pip ceremony poses: wipe/weigh, calipers argument, five-Pip pile-on, block-and-tackle on a lug.
G. Ammo row: Pea, Table berry, Cluster whole, Cluster breaking, Lug. Plus one pulp-burst impact.
H. GUI sheet: title lockup BUST A GRAPE; four crate-stencil lot buttons TABLE / PEA / CLUSTER / LUG; stain-bar power meter; turn chip “PIPS ON THE LIP”; announcer line “You couldn’t bust a grape in a food fight.”; win card “VINEGAR.”; three tiny HP bottles.

OUTPUT
- One master style board, landscape, labeled tiles, consistent lighting and line weight across every tile.
- Then a second page that is GUI-only, larger, as if it were a HUD sticker sheet.
- No screenshots of existing games. No logos from other companies. No photoreal vineyard tourism.

Voice check: if it looks like a tasting-room brochure or a kids’ fruit cartoon, reject and go dirtier and meaner.
```
